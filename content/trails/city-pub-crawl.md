This is a walk through the Square Mile and its western edge, where the old pubs survived because the streets around them never fully modernized: down alleys, behind courts, and tucked into the gaps between office blocks. It runs west to east for about 4 kilometers (2.5 miles), and the walking between stops adds up to about an hour.

Pace yourself. Have a half rather than a pint at each stop, and eat along the way: the Cheshire Cheese and Cittie of Yorke serve proper food, and the Mitre does toasties.

**Go on a weekday.** The Olde Wine Shades and the Jamaica Wine House are closed at weekends and the City empties out. On a Saturday, stop at the Black Friar, or end at the Hoop and Grapes.
