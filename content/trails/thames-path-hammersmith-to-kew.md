We've done this walk many times, with kids and with dogs, and it's the best way we know to spend a weekend day in West London. It starts at Hammersmith Bridge and runs along the north bank past a string of old riverside pubs, rowing clubs and Georgian houses. Then it crosses the Thames on the footway of Barnes Bridge and follows the towpath to Kew, finishing at [Kew Gardens](place:kew-gardens).

Do as much or as little as you like: the Hammersmith pubs alone make a lovely morning, Barnes makes a good half day, and Kew turns it into a full day. The route is flat. The north bank is mostly paved; the towpath beyond Barnes is unpaved and muddy after rain.

A few things to know before you set out:

- **Tides:** check tide times. At the highest spring tides the river floods parts of Chiswick Mall and the Mortlake towpath for an hour or two either side of high water.
- **Dogs:** they're welcome in every pub on the route, but [Kew Gardens](place:kew-gardens) admits only assistance dogs. With a dog in the group, finish at Barnes, or at the [Tap on the Line](place:tap-on-the-line) by Kew Gardens station.
- **Boat Race day:** in spring the whole route turns into a riverside party, and every pub is packed.
