London's best shopping isn't on Oxford Street. These are the markets, food shops and specialists we send people to, most of them small, old or both.

**Markets.** [Columbia Road](place:columbia-road-flower-market) is a Sunday-morning flower market along a Victorian East End street; go early. [Leadenhall Market](place:leadenhall-market) is a Victorian covered market built over Roman London's forum. For the truly early riser, [Billingsgate](place:billingsgate-market) is the country's largest inland fish market.

**Food and drink.** [The Hampstead Butcher & Providore](place:hampstead-butcher-and-providore) is a favorite for sausages and sausage rolls. [Kappacasein](place:kappacasein-dairy) makes raw-milk cheese in a Bermondsey railway arch, and [Kofali Hot Nuts](place:kofali-hot-nuts) on Green Lanes sells nuts warm from the roaster.

**Books, maps and antiques.** [Daunt Books](place:daunt-books-marylebone) in Marylebone, with its oak galleries and books shelved by country, is another favorite. [Word on the Water](place:word-on-the-water) is a bookshop on a barge at King's Cross, and [The Map House](place:the-map-house) is a lovely browse even if you're not buying. Antique hunters have [Alfies](place:alfies-antique-market) and [Grays](place:grays-antique-market), which began life as a showroom for toilets.

Check the days before you go: several of these keep short or unusual hours.
