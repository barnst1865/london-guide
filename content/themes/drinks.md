When you want something other than a pint, or a pint with something unexpected alongside it.

**Wine by candlelight.** [Gordon's Wine Bar](place:gordons-wine-bar) is one of our favorite bars anywhere: cellar vaults under Villiers Street, pouring wine, sherry and port since 1890. In the City, [the Olde Wine Shades](place:olde-wine-shades) is dark, wood-lined and old enough to be interesting.

**Beer at the source.** The tour at [Fuller's Griffin Brewery](place:fullers-griffin-brewery) in Chiswick ends with a tasting, and it fits neatly into our [Thames Path walk](trail:thames-path-hammersmith-to-kew).

**A drink with dinner.** [The Tamil Prince](place:tamil-prince) and [Three Falcons](place:three-falcons) are old London pubs reborn with Indian kitchens, so you can have a curry with your pint. For something smarter, [Sam's Riverside](place:sams-riverside) looks straight at Hammersmith Bridge and has a weekday oyster happy hour.

For the old pubs themselves, see [Historic Pubs & Restaurants](theme:historic-pubs).
