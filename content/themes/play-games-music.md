For evenings out, and for kids who've had enough museums. This theme is just getting started.

[Ain't Nothin' But](place:aint-nothin-but) is a tiny blues bar on Kingly Street with live music nearly every night. A few streets away, [Sounds of the Universe](place:sounds-of-the-universe) is a long-running Soho record shop with crates of used vinyl downstairs. And [Hamleys](place:hamleys) on Regent Street is touristy, but seven floors of toys make it a kid favorite for good reason.
