Most of medieval London burned in 1666 or went in the Blitz, but pieces of it survive if you know where to look, often down an alley you'd otherwise walk straight past.

[All Hallows-by-the-Tower](place:all-hallows-by-the-tower) is the oldest church in the City, and clearing its Blitz rubble uncovered a Saxon arch built of Roman tiles. [Ye Olde Mitre](place:ye-olde-mitre) hides down an alley off Hatton Garden on the grounds of a medieval bishop's palace. [Ye Olde Cheshire Cheese](place:ye-olde-cheshire-cheese), rebuilt soon after the Great Fire, is the pub we take everyone to first.
