London has been on screen and on record so often that you'll pass locations without knowing it. These are the ones worth a detour.

The [Abbey Road crossing](place:abbey-road-crossing) is the obvious one. It's a working road, so wait for a gap before you recreate the album cover. [Leadenhall Market](place:leadenhall-market) turns up in the first Harry Potter film, and fans of a different kind of classic can find the [Rickroll arches](place:rickroll-arches-freston-road) on Freston Road.

We have a soft spot for the Muppets. Jim Henson's London Creature Shop worked on [Oval Road](place:oval-road-henson-creature-shop) in Camden, his [blue plaque](place:jim-henson-blue-plaque) is in Hampstead, and you can visit three locations from The Great Muppet Caper: the [Happiness Hotel](place:westbourne-terrace-happiness-hotel), [Highbrow Street](place:holland-park-highbrow-street) and [Lady Holiday's office](place:chesham-place-lady-holiday).

Many of these are private homes and offices, so look from the pavement and keep it quick. For a sit-down afterward, [the Sherlock Holmes](place:sherlock-holmes-pub) pub has a replica of 221B's study upstairs.
