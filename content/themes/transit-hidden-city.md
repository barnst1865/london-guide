London's transport is a sight in itself, and some of the best of it is underground. This theme is just getting started.

The [London Transport Museum](place:london-transport-museum) in Covent Garden is our favorite rainy-day stop: two centuries of buses, trams and Tube trains, with a play zone for little kids. At the [Postal Museum](place:postal-museum) you ride a tiny driverless train through the old Mail Rail tunnels under Clerkenwell. And the [Brunel Museum](place:brunel-museum) in Rotherhithe sits at the start of the 1843 Thames Tunnel, which still carries Overground trains.
