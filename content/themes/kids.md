Every place in this guide is fair game with kids, but these are the ones that hold their attention. Most of the big museums here are free, which means you can leave when they've had enough and nobody's wasted a ticket.

**Museums that work for kids.** The [National Army Museum](place:national-army-museum) is a family favorite, with soft play for the under-8s and plenty for older kids. [HMS Belfast](place:hms-belfast) is a real warship to climb through, nine decks of it. The [RAF Museum](place:raf-museum-london) and the [Imperial War Museum](place:imperial-war-museum) are the places for kids who love planes and tanks.

**Running around.** The [Diana Memorial Playground](place:diana-memorial-playground) in [Kensington Gardens](place:kensington-gardens) has a pirate ship on a beach. [Hampstead Heath](place:hampstead-heath) and [Richmond Park](place:richmond-park) give them space and deer to chase (don't). [Kew Gardens](place:kew-gardens) has a treetop walkway.

**Feeding them.** [Tokyo Diner](place:tokyo-diner) is fast, cheap and easy with kids in tow. On the river, [Bread Street Kitchen in Limehouse](place:bread-street-kitchen-limehouse) was more kid-friendly than we expected.

Look for the **Kid-friendly** tag on any place page, and see [pub etiquette](guide:pub-etiquette) before taking kids to the pub.
