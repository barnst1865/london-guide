London is far greener than first-time visitors expect, and some of our best days here have had nothing to do with museums. This theme is for when you want to walk.

**The river.** Our favorite walk follows the [Thames Path from Hammersmith to Kew](trail:thames-path-hammersmith-to-kew), past rowing clubs and old riverside pubs like [the Dove](place:the-dove-hammersmith), and ends at [Kew Gardens](place:kew-gardens).

**The canal.** The [Regent's Canal](place:regents-canal-walk) from Little Venice to Camden is a towpath walk through a quieter London, past houseboats, garden villas and London Zoo.

**The big open spaces.** [Hampstead Heath](place:hampstead-heath) is a piece of countryside with a view of the whole city. Afterward, walk across it to [the Spaniards Inn](place:spaniards-inn). [Richmond Park](place:richmond-park) is bigger and wilder still, with hundreds of free-roaming deer.

**With younger kids.** [Kensington Gardens](place:kensington-gardens) has Peter Pan's statue and the [Diana Memorial Playground](place:diana-memorial-playground).

**A few tips for walking here:**

- **Weather:** London weather changes fast, so bring a layer and shoes that can cope with mud.
- **Travel:** most of these walks start and end near a station, so you never have to retrace your steps.
- **Dogs:** most parks welcome them, but check the rules where you're going. Kew Gardens and the playgrounds don't allow dogs, and Richmond Park has strict rules around the deer.
