Some things could only be in London. This is a small collection of them, and it will grow.

[Lock & Co.](place:lock-and-co-hatters) says it's the world's oldest hat shop, on St James's Street since 1765, and browsers are welcome. Nearby in Mayfair, [Annabel's](place:annabels) has been a members-only club since 1963, so it's a look from Berkeley Square. And for our American visitors, Benedict Arnold's [last London home](place:benedict-arnold-gloucester-place) on Gloucester Place carries a privately placed plaque calling him an "American Patriot".
