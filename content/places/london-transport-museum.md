Climb aboard real vehicles, from horse buses to Tube carriages, and see how the Underground grew with the city. The building began as a Victorian flower market.

**Our tip:** Booking ahead online is recommended but not required. Covent Garden station itself is not step-free, so use Leicester Square if that matters.
