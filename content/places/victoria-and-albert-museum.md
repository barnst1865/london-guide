It is the world's largest museum of applied arts, decorative arts and design, with more than 145 galleries. Several galleries are closed for works, including Fashion until 2028.

**Our tip:** Choose two or three galleries rather than trying to cover it all. Friday evenings run until 10pm, and the Exhibition Road colonnade carries Blitz shrapnel scars worth a look on the way out.
