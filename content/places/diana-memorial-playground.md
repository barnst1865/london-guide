The playground opened in 2000 in memory of Diana, Princess of Wales, and it's built around Peter Pan: a big wooden pirate galleon beached on sand, treehouses, a crocodile in the water-play area and a separate corner for the youngest. It closed for a full renewal in 2025 and reopened in August 2026 with a new ship and treehouse.

Entry is free. It's for children up to 12 and the adults with them; adults on their own can only look around during a short morning slot. No dogs except assistance dogs.

**Our tip:** Weekday mornings are calmest; sunny weekends and school holidays are busy. It's a few minutes' walk from Queensway station.
