Holmes was fictional, so the number is a courtesy: the council gave the house 221B in 1990, and three floors of Victorian rooms are recreated. Plan an hour or two.

**Our tip:** Book ahead and avoid peak tourist seasons if you can. There's no lift, and the stairs are narrow.
