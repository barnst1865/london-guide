Lore of the Land feels like the newest pub in this theme, but it isn't. It occupies a late-18th-century corner building near Fitzroy Square that has been a pub since 1829, under a string of names: the Adam's Arms for most of its life, later O'Neill's and then the Lukin. Film director Guy Ritchie bought it and reopened it in 2019, with a handsome refit and a dining room upstairs, and it has become one of our favorites.

It's a calmer spot than the busy West End pubs, and an easy stop if you're near Regent's Park or the BT Tower. The Sunday roast is the big draw, and it books up well ahead.

**Our tip:** Book for Sunday lunch, and book early.
