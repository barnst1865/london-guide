It is an independent venue that has been running since 1993, named after a Georgia White blues song. Seating is mostly at the back and the stage is small.

**Our tip:** Weekend afternoons have early sets if you would rather catch music before the late crowd. Wednesday to Saturday evenings may carry a cover charge, and we haven't confirmed the age policy.
