Opened in January 2021, this little Primrose Hill shop is styled like an American soda parlor from the 1950s. Seating is limited, and it has reportedly offered ice cream for dogs.

**Our tip:** It's closed Monday and Tuesday and may cut its days in winter, so check before you make the trip.
