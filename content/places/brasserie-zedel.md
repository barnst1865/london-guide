Walk in off a side street by Piccadilly Circus, go down the stairs, and you're in one of the grandest rooms in the West End: marble, gilt, pillars and chandeliers, buzzing like a Paris brasserie. This was the basement of the Regent Palace Hotel, which opened in 1915. Its 1930s interiors are listed, and they were restored when Brasserie Zédel opened here in 2012.

The surprise is the price. The French classics (carottes râpées, steak haché with peppercorn sauce, choucroute, crème brûlée) are done well rather than brilliantly, but the set menu is a bargain for a room like this. It's a good place for a group, a family dinner or visiting parents. The Bar Américain and the Crazy Coqs cabaret room are in the same basement.

**Our tip:** Order from the prix fixe and spend the savings on wine. Book for evenings and weekends; it's big, but it fills up.
