A French patisserie, bakery and cafe that opened in 2002. It sells pastries, quiches, bread and cakes, and also stocks French imports. The building, at 36 to 37 Newington Green, is Grade II listed. It's part of a small group of shops.

**Our tip:** There's no Tube station on the green, so allow time for a walk or a bus. Check the hours before you go.
