A 1920s Dutch barge that has been a bookshop since 2011, with a permanent mooring on the canal at Granary Square. In warmer months there are music and poetry events on the roof.

**Our tip:** Access is by a narrow gangway and it's small. It can close at short notice, so don't make it the only plan.
