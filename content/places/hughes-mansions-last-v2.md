At 7:21 on the morning of 27 March 1945, with the war in Europe six weeks from its end, a V-2 rocket fell on the middle block of Hughes Mansions, three blocks of council flats on Vallance Road built in the 1920s. It killed 134 people, around 120 of them from the Jewish families who made up most of the East End community here, and seriously injured 49 more. It was the last rocket to hit London, and one of the last two to hit England; the other fell on Orpington the same afternoon and killed the last British civilian of the war.

The estate was rebuilt, and in the communal garden behind it, off Vallance Road, a plaque on a stone plinth records the dead and the exact minute. Two streets away, Vollasky House is named for a local alderman killed in the attack. The Jewish East End Celebration Society still holds anniversary commemorations here.

This is people's homes. The plaque is visible from the estate path; go quietly, do not photograph windows, and give it the five minutes it deserves.

**Our tip:** It is a ten-minute walk from the [Bethnal Green memorial](place:bethnal-green-stairway-to-heaven), which makes a short, serious East End pairing; Whitechapel station is nearest.
