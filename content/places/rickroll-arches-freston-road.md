Londonist and others place the video's underpass scene under the Hammersmith & City viaduct on Freston Road. The exact arch isn't confirmed, so the pin is approximate. These are working railway arches: we stay on the pavement. The indoor scenes were shot at a nearby youth club, the Harrow Club, which is why many captions wrongly say Harrow.

**Our tip:** Nothing marks the spot, so it's a look-and-leave stop. Latimer Road is the closest station.
