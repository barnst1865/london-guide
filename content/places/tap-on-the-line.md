Kew Gardens station opened in 1869, and the Tap on the Line occupies one of its original yellow-brick buildings. It has only been a pub since around the 1980s, under a string of names, and Fuller's reopened it as the Tap on the Line in 2013. It's often called the only pub on a London Underground platform. The seats outside look onto the District line and Overground trains, which small children tend to find more exciting than anything on the menu.

Families and dogs are welcome. On our walk it's where you arrive after leaving the river, five minutes before Kew Gardens' Victoria Gate, and it's a good place to wait for the train home afterward.

**Our tip:** With a dog in the group, this is the place to regroup: Kew Gardens doesn't admit pet dogs, so decide here who's going in.
