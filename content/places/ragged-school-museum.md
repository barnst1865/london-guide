The museum fills three warehouses beside the Regent's Canal that held London's largest ragged school until 1908. A recreated Victorian classroom is the centerpiece.

**Our tip:** On the first Sunday of the month, arrive early for the classroom lesson (first come, first served). Check the site for current opening days.
