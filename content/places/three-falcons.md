From at least the 1870s this corner of Lisson Grove was the Richmond Arms, a backstreet local; the present building went up in the 1920s as part of a housing block. In 2019 it reopened as the Three Falcons, an Indian gastropub with hotel rooms upstairs, and it's one of the more unexpected meals in this part of town.

The kitchen cooks North Indian food (all the meat is halal) alongside a short list of pub classics, so mixed groups are easy: Old Delhi butter chicken, Kashmiri rogan josh, lamb chops and crisp okra. There's sport on the TVs, which makes it noisy on match days but fun if you want to catch a game. It's a short walk from Lord's, the Regent's Canal and Little Venice.

**Our tip:** Book on a cricket or football day, or come midweek for a quieter room. Order the butter chicken.
