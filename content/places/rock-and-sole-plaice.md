The London Museum says this chip shop has served from the same spot since 1871, which would make it one of the oldest in the city. It was reportedly rebuilt and renamed after the Second World War, so think of it as a long-running site more than a preserved Victorian one. Fish and chips, with tables inside and out.

**Our tip:** Opening hours differ between sources, so check before you go.
