Hamleys says it began as 'Noah's Ark' on High Holborn in 1760; it has been on Regent Street since 1881, and in its current building since 1981. Expect demonstrators, magic-trick counters and crowds.

**Our tip:** It is busiest around Christmas. Outside the holiday rush it is far easier to browse, and entry is free.
