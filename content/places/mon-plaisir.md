Mon Plaisir opened in 1943 and has been a Covent Garden bistro ever since. The Lauro family took over in 2022 after about fifty years under the previous owner. The story goes that the bar's zinc top came from a Lyon brothel, but we haven't found a source, so treat that as a tale.

**Our tip:** Look at the set menus when you book.
