The obelisk was put up around 1740 and moved a few hundred yards about a century later. It stands on the edge of Hadley Green beside a busy road. The story goes it marks the spot where Warwick fell, but nobody knows the original position.

**Our tip:** It is a short stop on the northern edge of Greater London, so pair it with a walk on Hadley Green rather than making a special trip. Buses from High Barnet serve the green.
