A fish restaurant on St Martin's Court, a lane off Charing Cross Road, that has traded on the same site since 1896. It's a smart room rather than a casual one, with a crustacean bar for oysters and shellfish.

**Our tip:** Sheekey's Oyster Bar next door is the option if you want something smaller.
