On 1 November 2006, former FSB officer Alexander Litvinenko met two Russians in the Pine Bar of what was then the Millennium Hotel, and drank tea laced with polonium-210. He died three weeks later. The hotel reopened in 2019 as The Biltmore Mayfair.

**Our tip:** The square itself is worth a look. It was the heart of the American presence in London for decades.
