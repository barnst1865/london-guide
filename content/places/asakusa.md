It's on our list because of one line in a 2011 local review: "about 80 per cent of the customers seemed to be Japanese." Asakusa is an izakaya, a Japanese pub-restaurant built for drinking and sharing small plates, and it has sat on Eversholt Street for more than thirty years. A Japanese travel site calls it London's real-deal izakaya, with Japanese-speaking staff and a handwritten menu, and the Camden New Journal's 2024 visit found that not much had changed.

It's a ten-minute walk from Euston, so it works for anyone arriving by train from the north.

**Our tip:** Order several small plates and a beer rather than one big main. Some recent reviewers felt the bill crept up, so keep an eye on it.
