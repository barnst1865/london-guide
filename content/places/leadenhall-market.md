Sir Horace Jones designed this ornate covered market, which opened in 1881. It stands on the site of Roman London's forum and basilica, and a market has been here since the 14th century by the market's own account. Its cobbled passages were used in the first Harry Potter film.

**Our tip:** Come on a weekday: at weekends the City is quiet and many of the shops and restaurants are shut.
