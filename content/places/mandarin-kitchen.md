It's on our list for one dish: lobster noodles, egg noodles braised with lobster, ginger and spring onion. Mandarin Kitchen has been cooking them on Queensway since 1978, and a 2018 food-forum post called it the go-to for "HKers, Singaporeans and Malaysians visiting London for the past 3 decades or so."

This isn't a cheap eat. The bill adds up quickly, so treat it as the splurge on this list. The dining room is plain; the food is the point.

**Our tip:** Book, come with at least three or four people, and order the lobster noodles to share, then fill in with greens and rice.
