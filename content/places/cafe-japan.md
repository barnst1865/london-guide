A casual Japanese cafe on Finchley Road, run by the Atariya group, with sushi and set meals. A 2013 Time Out review noted its claim to have invented deep-fried inside-out rolls, so take that as the cafe's own boast.

**Our tip:** Days and hours vary, so check the booking page before you set out.
