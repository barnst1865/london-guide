It's on our list because this is where London's love affair with the ocakbaşı, the Turkish open charcoal grill, more or less began. Ali Dirik opened Mangal on a Dalston side street at the turn of the 1990s, and an early-'90s Time Out review still hangs in the window. You sit near the grill and watch the lamb chops and kebabs go over the coals.

It's cheap, smoky and busy most nights. It's also the original "Mangal 1," not its now-fashionable offshoot, Mangal 2, around the corner.

**Our tip:** It's bring-your-own, so pick up a bottle on Kingsland High Street. Order a mixed grill to share.
