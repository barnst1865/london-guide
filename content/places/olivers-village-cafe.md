Oliver's is a tiny café on a village-y stretch of Belsize Lane, and it's the kind of place we wish every neighborhood had. Oliver, an artist, set it up in 2008 partly as somewhere to work and show his paintings, which still cover the walls. At the back is a skylit room full of plants and cushions, and there's a corner with books and crayons for children.

The food is simple and generous: full English and vegetarian breakfasts, eggs on avocado toast, shakshuka, pandan cake and, on Sundays, a roast. Dogs are welcome too. It isn't somewhere to cross London for, but if you're spending a morning in Hampstead, Belsize Park or Primrose Hill, it's a friendly, inexpensive stop.

**Our tip:** Order Oliver's Posh Breakfast and take the back room if it's free.
