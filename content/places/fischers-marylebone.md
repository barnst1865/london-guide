A Viennese cafe-restaurant and cake shop that Chris Corbin and Jeremy King opened on Marylebone High Street in 2014, styled after an old Vienna cafe. It's now part of the Wolseley Hospitality Group. Come for coffee and strudel as much as for schnitzel.
