Mail Rail carried letters under London from 1927 until 2003, and a short stretch now takes passengers in small open carriages. The main museum covers stamps, pillar boxes and two centuries of the post.

**Our tip:** Book a Mail Rail slot ahead, especially on weekends, and allow a couple of hours for both buildings.
