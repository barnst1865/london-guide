This is a working wholesale market, not a tourist attraction, and that is the draw: about a hundred stands of fish and shellfish, busiest in the early morning. The City of London Corporation says the public can visit, and individuals can buy small amounts.

**Our tip:** Go early, wear non-slip shoes, and leave wheeled bags behind. Under-12s aren't allowed on the market floor, and photography needs advance permission.
