A pub in a converted row of shops on Kew Green, a few minutes from Kew Gardens. It's reported to have been a butcher's shop, with part of the old interior kept. There are tables on the pavement and in a courtyard at the back, and it serves a Sunday set menu.

**Our tip:** The Green is a pleasant place to wait for a table on a dry day.
