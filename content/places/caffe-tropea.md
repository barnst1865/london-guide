A family-run Italian cafe that has long traded from inside Russell Square gardens. It's self-service, with seating inside and out, and sells coffee, light meals and gelato. The gardens are a short walk from the British Museum.

**Our tip:** Sit outside on a dry day. The gardens themselves are a good place to let children run around.
