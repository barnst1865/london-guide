Sam's Riverside is a smart, buzzy brasserie at the foot of Hammersmith Bridge, part of the rebuilt Riverside Studios arts center, and the floor-to-ceiling windows look straight onto the bridge's green-and-gold ironwork and the river. Sam Harrison, a west London restaurateur who spent years working with Rick Stein, opened it in 2019.

A full dinner here is a treat rather than a bargain. Our reason for sending people is the oyster happy hour on weekday evenings, when oysters are cheap and you can pair a dozen with a glass of English sparkling wine and watch the rowers go by. It's a good start to an evening walk along the Thames Path, upstream to the pubs of Hammersmith Mall such as [The Dove](place:the-dove-hammersmith).

**Our tip:** Check the venue's site for the oyster happy hour times (weekdays, early evening) and sit by the window.
