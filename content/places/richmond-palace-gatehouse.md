Walk to the corner of the Green to see the gatehouse and its royal arms, then the entrance to Old Palace Yard. The gatehouse and the neighboring Wardrobe are private homes today, so we look from the street and the Green.

**Our tip:** Fold it into a loop around Richmond Green, and keep to the street and the Green.
