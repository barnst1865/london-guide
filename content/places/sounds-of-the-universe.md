Founder Stuart Baker started on a market stall and moved through Soho before settling on Broadwick Street. Expect everything from reggae and soul to Brazilian and hip-hop.

**Our tip:** Head down to the basement for the second-hand vinyl; it pairs neatly with a stop at the blues bar on Kingly Street.
