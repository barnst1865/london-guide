The 1884 conference that chose Greenwich as the world's zero was held in Washington, DC, which makes this a good one for an American family. The Cutty Sark and the free museums down the hill are a short walk away.

**Our tip:** Check the Royal Museums Greenwich site before you travel: the observatory is shut from 2 November 2026, and only the Meridian Courtyard is promised back before the full reopening in spring 2028.
