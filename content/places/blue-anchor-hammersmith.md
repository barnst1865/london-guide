Step off Hammersmith Bridge onto Lower Mall and the Blue Anchor is the first pub you reach, which is why the Thames Path walk starts here. It's a narrow, wood-paneled room with a handful of tables outside on the riverside path, where you can watch the rowing clubs carry their boats down to the water.

The pub says it was first licensed in 1722. The earliest record historians have found is from 1843, which still makes it one of the old-timers on this stretch; the building itself is mid-19th century. It has had a small screen career too, in *Sliding Doors* and the opening credits of *Minder*. The claim that Gustav Holst wrote his Hammersmith music here is one of those stories pubs like to tell; nobody has shown it's true.

**Our tip:** Get an outside table on a weekend morning and watch the crews go out. The Rutland is next door and the Dove is five minutes up the Mall.
