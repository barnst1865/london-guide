It's on our list because Isaan, the northeast of Thailand, has its own food (sour, fiery and built around sticky rice) and Esarn Kheaw has been cooking it on the Uxbridge Road since 1992. Mr and Mrs Puntar opened it, and the website still greets visitors in Thai before English. Reviewers describe a room of locals and expats, with a couple of "aunties" steering newcomers through the menu.

It's a plain dining room on a busy road, not a destination, and that's the appeal: cheap, specific and run by the family that started it.

**Our tip:** Ask what to order, and be honest about how much heat you can take. Papaya salad and sticky rice are the place to start.
