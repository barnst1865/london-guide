The Regency Cafe looks like a film set because it often is one: the black-tiled 1940s exterior and the cream-and-brown interior have turned up in *Layer Cake*, *Pride* and *Rocketman*, among others. It opened in 1946 on a quiet street between Westminster and Pimlico, and it's still a classic London greasy spoon: plain tables, big plates and quick service.

Order a full English, a bacon sandwich or one of the daily specials, such as steak pie, and a mug of builder's tea (strong, with milk). The long-serving family sold it in 2025, and the new owners have kept it going. Prices have crept up since, but it's still a cheap, filling and very London start to a day around Westminster.

**Our tip:** Go early for breakfast, before a morning at Westminster or the Churchill War Rooms. It's closed on Sundays, and there's often a queue at lunchtime.
