Dishoom is a chain, and normally we'd steer you away from chains, but it's a good one and visitors always ask about it. It's modeled on the old Irani cafés of Bombay, and the central London branches have famously long queues. This branch, which opened in 2023 inside the restored Battersea Power Station, is the one we use: it's huge, and it's usually much easier to get a table here.

It's open from breakfast to late. Come in the morning for a bacon naan roll and chai, or later for black dal, chicken ruby, lamb chops and okra fries. It's an easy place to bring kids, and it's the first Dishoom to let dogs in. Afterward, wander around the Power Station and along the river.

**Our tip:** Book ahead for daytime tables (any size) or if you're a group of six or more in the evening; smaller groups after 6pm walk in.
