Violet's was an unexpected delight: a small café-restaurant on Berwick Street, the street-market end of Soho, cooking the food of Georgia, the country in the Caucasus. If you haven't tried Georgian food, it's generous and very shareable: khachapuri (boat-shaped bread filled with molten cheese and often an egg), khinkali (big twisted soup dumplings), aubergine rolled around walnut paste, and slow-cooked lamb stews heavy with herbs.

It's named after Violet Trayte, the Soho bingo caller once known as the Queen of Soho. It's a casual, cozy room rather than a destination restaurant, and it suits a lunch or an early dinner between the shops and the theaters.

**Our tip:** Share an Adjarian khachapuri (the boat-shaped one) and a plate of khinkali. Eat the dumplings by hand, holding the twisted top, and sip the broth out first.
