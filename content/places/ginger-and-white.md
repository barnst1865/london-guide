A small cafe tucked into Perrins Court, a narrow lane off Hampstead High Street. It opened in 2009, changed owners in 2019, and is now a single cafe. Tables are inside and outside, and there's no booking.

**Our tip:** Weekends are busy and the inside is tiny, so go early or on a weekday.
