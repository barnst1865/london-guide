Liberty was founded on Regent Street in 1875. This mock-Tudor building on Great Marlborough Street dates from 1922-24, and the story is that its timbers came from two old warships. It is still known for its prints and fabrics.

**Our tip:** The main entrance is on Great Marlborough Street.
