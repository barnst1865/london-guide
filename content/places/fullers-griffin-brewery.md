Where Chiswick Mall meets the main road, the Griffin Brewery is still making London Pride and ESB. By the brewery's own account there has been brewing on this site since the late 1600s; the Fuller, Smith & Turner partnership dates from 1845. In 2019 the Japanese brewer Asahi bought the brewing business while Fuller's kept its pubs, but the beer is still brewed here.

Tours take you through the brewhouse and finish with a tasting in the old cellars. They're for over-18s only, involve several flights of stairs, and need booking ahead. Look for the huge wisteria on the front of the building, which Fuller's says was planted in 1816 and is the oldest in England. That's the company's story rather than a verified record. The brewery shop and tap room don't need a booking.

**Our tip:** Book a tour for the middle of the walk and let the rest of the group carry on along Chiswick Mall, or just stop at the shop for a bottle to take home.
