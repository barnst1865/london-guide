The signature here is the triple hot chocolate, which comes in milk, gianduia and dark versions. It's a small group with two shops, this one on Broadwick Street. Queues are common, and tables can be booked.

**Our tip:** Expect a queue. A weekday morning is easier, and you can book a table through the website.
