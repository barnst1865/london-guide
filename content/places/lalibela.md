It's on our list because eating Ethiopian food is a good experience to share with visitors: everyone eats from one big platter, tearing off pieces of spongy injera bread to scoop up stews and tibs. Lalibela has been doing it in Tufnell Park since the early 1990s, across two small floors.

Reviewers single out the traditional coffee ceremony served at the table. It's cheap, and it's a short walk from Tufnell Park station, so it pairs well with an afternoon on Hampstead Heath.

**Our tip:** Order a mixed platter to share so you can try several dishes, and ask for the coffee ceremony at the end.
