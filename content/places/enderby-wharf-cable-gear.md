Cable for the 1865 and 1866 transatlantic telegraph attempts was made on this wharf and laid by the Great Eastern. The machinery you see is later, last used in 1978, and was made a Scheduled Monument in 2025.

**Our tip:** Walk it on the Thames Path between the O2 and Greenwich, and finish with a drink at Enderby House next door. Both stations are a fair walk (roughly 20 minutes).
