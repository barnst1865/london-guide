Tucked behind the Royal Courts of Justice, the Seven Stars is small, crooked and full of character, and a favorite with the lawyers who work around it. The front says 1602; CAMRA's pub historians think the building more likely dates from the 1680s, with an extension added in 1878. Inside, the Victorian fittings survive, including etched doors marked "Private Counter" and "General Counter," which may be the only ones of their kind.

Since 2001 it has been run by Roxy Beaujolais, a former TV presenter and cookbook author, and the food is a cut above the pub standard. Then there are the cats. The pub has kept a line of resident cats, starting with Tom Paine; the current one, the General, arrived in 2021 and wears an Elizabethan ruff.

**Our tip:** It doesn't take bookings and space is tight, so come at an off-peak hour. The restrooms are up a steep, winding staircase.
