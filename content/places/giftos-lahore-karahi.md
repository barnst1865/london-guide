It's on our list because it's exactly the kind of place we look for: a Pakistani grill house that has fed Southall's Lahori community for three decades, not a dining room built for visitors. Gifto's started in 1968 as a grocery serving the neighbourhood and became a full restaurant in 1994. Reviews describe big family groups, iftar parties during Ramadan and catering for family celebrations, and the noise level to match.

Southall is a trip, about 20 minutes from Paddington on the Elizabeth line, but The Broadway is worth a wander anyway for its sari shops, sweet centres and grocers.

**Our tip:** Go hungry and in a group. The lamb chops and the karahis are what people come back for.
