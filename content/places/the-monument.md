The column is 202 feet tall because that is its distance from the Pudding Lane bakery where the 1666 fire started. It is a tight spiral staircase with no lift, so we would say it suits kids who like a climb.

**Our tip:** Tickets are bought on the day at the door, so just turn up. Term-time weekday mornings are busy with school groups.
