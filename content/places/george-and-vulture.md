A few steps from the Jamaica Wine House, in the same maze of alleys, the George and Vulture is a traditional City chophouse in an 18th-century building that has been a pub, coffee house and chophouse in turn. Dickens made it Mr. Pickwick's London base in *The Pickwick Papers*, and Dickens societies still meet here.

The claim that an inn has stood on this spot since 1142 is legend, and so is the story that the Hellfire Club met here. It's a Samuel Smith's house, so expect hearty chops and grills at lunchtime and the brewery's house rules.

**Our tip:** Go for a weekday lunch, and check it's open before you set out.
