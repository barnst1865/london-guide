Simpson’s Tavern, traditionally founded in 1757, closed in 2022 after a rent dispute. The same Grade II building, an early-1700s City tavern, is now Cloth Cornhill, with a bar downstairs and a restaurant above. It runs on weekday City hours, so don’t plan it for a weekend.
