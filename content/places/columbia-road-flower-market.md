From early morning every Sunday, traders fill the street with cut flowers, bedding plants and shrubs. The Victorian shopfronts behind the stalls are independent shops and cafes, and the street gets very busy by mid-morning.

**Our tip:** Go early, before the crowds, and bring a bag for plants. It's Sundays only.
