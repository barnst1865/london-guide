The film link is thin. The Admiral Boom connection comes from the Mary Poppins books and is popular tradition, not documented, and the 1964 film was made at Disney's studio in Burbank, not here. What is real: a naval captain added the roof deck in the late 1700s, and the architect George Gilbert Scott lived in the house in the 1850s and early 1860s. These are private homes, so we look from the lane and carry on to the Heath.

**Our tip:** Pair it with a stroll up Hampstead Grove and onto Hampstead Heath, and keep to the lane.
