It's on our list because it's an easy, honest meal two minutes from Leicester Square, which is rarer than it should be. Tokyo Diner opened in 1992 in a former launderette on the edge of Chinatown and has served Japanese home cooking (curry rice, bento, noodles) ever since. All the staff speak Japanese, and the house policy has always been no tipping.

It's a good fallback before or after a West End show, and the menu is approachable if you're new to Japanese food.

**Our tip:** Japanese curry with a breaded cutlet is the comfort-food order. Don't leave a tip; they mean it.
