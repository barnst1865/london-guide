In 1854 Dr John Snow mapped cholera deaths around a water pump on what was then Broad Street, and persuaded the parish to remove its handle. The replica (handle-less, of course) stands next to the John Snow pub.

**Our tip:** A five-minute stop on a wander through Soho.
