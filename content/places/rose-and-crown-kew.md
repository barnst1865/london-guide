Kew Green is the village end of Kew: a big triangle of grass with a cricket pitch, an old church, and the gardens' Elizabeth Gate at the far side. The Rose & Crown looks across it. A pub is recorded here from at least 1860. It was the Rose and Crown until 2013, then the Cricketers, and new owners brought back the old name after refurbishing it in 2025.

It's the stop for the long version of our walk. Stay on the towpath past the turn for the station, carry on to Kew Bridge, then cut across the green.

**Our tip:** On a summer weekend, sit out front and watch the cricket. Kew Bridge station is just across the bridge.
