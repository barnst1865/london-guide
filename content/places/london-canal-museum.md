The building went up around 1862-63 for ice-cream maker Carlo Gatti to store ice shipped from Norway; the museum opened here in 1992.

**Our tip:** Open Tuesday to Sunday with no booking needed. In boat-trip season, book a canal trip separately and walk the towpath afterward.
