A family bakery that says it has been baking since 1820 and in Crouch End since 1946. The 1820 date is the bakery's own account, and some sources give 1850. It sells bread, doughnuts and cream cakes. There's a second shop in Muswell Hill.

**Our tip:** Both shops use a ticket system for the queue, so take a number.
