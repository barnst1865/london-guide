Sir George Frampton's bronze stands at the water's edge, with fairies and small animals at child height around the trunk. The fairies-did-it story was J. M. Barrie's own publicity line.

**Our tip:** It is a short walk in from Lancaster Gate, and an easy add-on to the Diana Memorial Playground.
