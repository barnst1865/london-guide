A small bakery in Colonnade, a quiet passage off Brunswick Square, that opened in March 2018 and specializes in slow-fermented bakes, filled danishes and cakes. There is very little seating, so it works best as a takeaway stop.

**Our tip:** Go early. It sells out by the early afternoon.
