"Desi pubs" are a very British institution: ordinary pubs taken over by South Asian (usually Punjabi) landlords from the 1960s onward, mostly in the Midlands, serving sizzling mixed grills and curries alongside the beer. The Tamil Prince is a modern London take on the idea. Prince Durairaj, a chef from Tamil Nadu, and Glen Leeson took over a 19th-century pub in Barnsbury in 2022, kept the bar and the pints, and filled the kitchen with South Indian cooking.

Order flaky roti, okra fries and onion bhajis to start, then a Chettinad lamb curry or grilled prawns, with chana bhatura (chickpeas with puffed fried bread) if you're hungry. It's in the Michelin Guide and was named one of Britain's top 50 gastropubs in 2025, so it's busy. Their second pub, the Tamil Crown, is near Angel.

**Our tip:** Book, especially on weekends. It's a pub, so you can also just drop in for a drink and a plate of okra fries at the bar.
