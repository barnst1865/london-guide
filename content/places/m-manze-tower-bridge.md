Pie and mash is the old working-class London meal: a minced-beef pie, mashed potato and a green parsley sauce called liquor. The shop has been on this spot since the early 1890s and in the Manze family since 1902. It's one of the last of its kind, and the place to try jellied eels if you're curious.

**Our tip:** Order the pie and mash with liquor. The eels are optional.
