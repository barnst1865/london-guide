A rustic, candlelit restaurant in a side street off Kensington Church Street, with several small rooms over two floors. It opened as Nan's Kitchen in the 1960s, closed after a fire in 2023, and reopened in November 2025. Portions are generous.

**Our tip:** Book ahead, and ask about the ground-floor rooms if stairs are a problem.
