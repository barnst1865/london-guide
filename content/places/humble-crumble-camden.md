A takeaway stall in the Stables Market at Camden, part of a small London group that began in 2018. The menu is built around hot fruit crumbles, which you can have with custard or topped with marshmallow that's toasted with a blowtorch.

**Our tip:** Take it away and eat it warm. It's a busy market, so expect crowds at the weekend.
