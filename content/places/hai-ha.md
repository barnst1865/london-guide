It's on our list because of a detail in a Time Out review: on a fine day the pavement tables are "usually occupied by groups of Vietnamese men, drinking and talking." Hackney has one of London's oldest Vietnamese communities, and Hai Ha cooks northern Vietnamese dishes you don't often see in London.

The takeaway counter leans toward familiar Chinese dishes; the sit-down menu is where the Vietnamese cooking is. It's a short walk from Hackney Central and an easy add-on to a Saturday at Broadway Market or London Fields.

**Our tip:** Order the cha ca la vong (turmeric-marinated fish with dill and peanuts) or the seafood bun hue noodle soup.
