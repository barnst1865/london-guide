He lived here from 1837 to 1839, and the rooms are laid out as a working Victorian household. There is a tea room and a shop on site.

**Our tip:** Open Wednesday to Sunday. Book a timed slot ahead, and leave bags and pushchairs behind: the house is small and the stairs are steep.
