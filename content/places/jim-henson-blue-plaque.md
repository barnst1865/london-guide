English Heritage unveiled the plaque in 2021 to the creator of the Muppets. It marks a private home, so the pin sits on the street and we read the plaque from the pavement.

**Our tip:** Downshire Hill runs down toward Keats Grove and Hampstead Heath, so it fits into a Hampstead walk.
