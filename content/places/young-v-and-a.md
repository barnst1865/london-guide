Three galleries (Imagine, Play, Design) mix toys, dressing up and making spaces. There is a cafe and free lockers.

**Our tip:** Free with no booking, so it works as a short stop. Bethnal Green station is a two-minute walk but isn't step-free; the museum names Whitechapel as the nearest step-free Tube.
