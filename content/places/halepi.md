It's on our list because Halepi is where Greek-Cypriot London began: it opened on this Bayswater street in 1966 as the city's first Greek-Cypriot restaurant, and a plaque inside marks the date. A Greek food writer describes it as recreating, for UK Cypriots, the rustic tavernas and community-hall celebrations of their childhoods, and Cypriot reviewers still vouch for it.

It costs more than most places on this list, so treat it as a proper night out. It's a few minutes from Hyde Park's Italian Gardens.

**Our tip:** Come with a group and order the souvla and kleftiko (slow-cooked lamb) to share. It stays open late.
