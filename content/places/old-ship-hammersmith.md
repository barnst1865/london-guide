The Old Ship sits at the western end of Upper Mall with a riverside terrace, a covered veranda and an upstairs balcony looking over the Thames. A Ship Inn was here by 1795 (the Survey of London found it in the manor court rolls), but the pub was rebuilt around 1850 and modernized since, so it's the spot rather than the interior that's historic.

It's a Young's pub and a reliable lunch stop on the walk: plenty of outdoor seating, families and dogs welcome, and a good view of the rowing from the balcony.

**Our tip:** If the Dove's tiny terrace is full, walk five minutes west. The Old Ship almost always has room outside.
