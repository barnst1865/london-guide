Thomas Coram's charity hospital became an unlikely art gallery when Hogarth and other artists donated work to help raise money. Handel gave benefit concerts for it, and the museum holds a major Handel collection.

**Our tip:** The museum warns of partial or full closures on some dates this autumn and over the holidays, so check its site before you go.
