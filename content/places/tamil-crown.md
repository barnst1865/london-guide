The second venue from the team behind [The Tamil Prince](place:tamil-prince), opened in December 2023 in what had been the Charles Lamb pub. Like its sister, it's a modern take on the desi pub, with South Indian cooking. The downstairs bar takes walk-ins and the upstairs room is more formal. An Indian Sunday roast is a feature.

**Our tip:** Book the upstairs dining room. The bar downstairs is the place to drop in.
