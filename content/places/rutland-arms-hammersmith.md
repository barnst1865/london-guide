When the Blue Anchor is full, and on a sunny afternoon it will be, the Rutland next door has room to spare. A pub has stood here since 1849; the present building went up around 1870 as the Rutland Hotel and lost its top floor and balcony in the Blitz.

Rowing sculls hang from the ceiling, a nod to the clubs that launch from the Mall, and the riverside tables look straight back down the river to Hammersmith Bridge. It's a Greene King pub, so the menu is standard chain fare; the setting is the reason to come. Families and dogs are welcome, which makes it an easy first stop with a group.

**Our tip:** Boat Race day packs this stretch of Lower Mall solid. Any other weekend it's a relaxed place for a first drink before you walk west.
