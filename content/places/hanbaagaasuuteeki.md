A drop-in burger place with high stools and counter tables, a few minutes from Victoria station, that opened in 2025. It serves smashed-beef burgers with Asian flavors such as kimchi and Thai-inspired toppings, plus miso fries. The name is a Japanese-style spelling of 'hamburger steak'.
