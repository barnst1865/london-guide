A neighborhood restaurant, deli and takeaway from the Ottolenghi group, a short walk up Hill Street from Richmond station. The counter sells salads, cakes and bakes to take away, and there is a sit-down restaurant as well.

**Our tip:** Take a salad or a pastry to the river or the park if the weather is good.
