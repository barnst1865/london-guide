Ida is the neighborhood Italian everyone wishes they had. Avi and Simonetta Wenkert opened it in 2007 in a small corner shop in Queen's Park and named it after Avi's mother, Ida, who was born in the Marche, in central Italy. Most of the cooking comes from her recipes, Avi is in the kitchen, and their grown-up children help run the place.

The menu is short and the pasta is made fresh: ragù from the Marche over pappardelle, gnocchi, amatriciana, then torta della nonna or tiramisu. It's warm, a bit crowded, and good value for cooking this careful. In 2025 the Good Food Guide named it the best local restaurant in London. It's out of the center, but only a short ride on the Bakerloo line or the Overground.

**Our tip:** Book well ahead: it's small, with timed sittings. It's dinner Tuesday to Saturday plus Sunday lunch, and there's no room for buggies.
