Simpson's began in 1828 as the Grand Cigar Divan, a smoking room and coffee house where people played chess, and became a tavern and restaurant from 1848. Roast beef carved at the table from silver trolleys is the tradition; the story goes that the trolleys were introduced so the carving wouldn't disturb the chess players. It closed in 2020 and reopened in March 2026 after a restoration, so it's a revival, not unbroken service.

**Our tip:** Book ahead. It reopened only this year, so check the current menu.
