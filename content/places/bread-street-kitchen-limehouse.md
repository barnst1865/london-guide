We don't usually send people to chain restaurants, but this one earns its place for its setting. It's in the old customs house at the entrance to Limehouse Basin, a listed Edwardian building where the Regent's Canal meets the Thames. For years it was Gordon Ramsay's gastropub The Narrow, and it's now one of his Bread Street Kitchens.

The food is solid pub-restaurant cooking (burgers, beef Wellington, a Sunday roast) rather than anything special. What makes it is the conservatory and terrace right on the river, and the fact that it was much better with our kids than we expected: there's a children's menu and high chairs. It's an easy stop on a Thames walk through Limehouse and Wapping. Grown-ups can have a drink afterward at [The Grapes](place:the-grapes-limehouse) along the same street.

**Our tip:** Book a table in the conservatory or on the terrace if the weather's good, and time it for a walk along the Thames Path.
