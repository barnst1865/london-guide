Cheese is made here in a railway arch in the Alpine style, from raw milk, which makes it one of the few cheesemakers still working in London. The Bermondsey shop is open on Saturdays; the toasties and raclette that made the name are sold at its Borough Market stall.

**Our tip:** Check the website before you go: the Bermondsey shop keeps short hours.
