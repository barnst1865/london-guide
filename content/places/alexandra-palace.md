The park is free and open to everyone, with a play area, a boating lake and an ice rink. The old BBC studios in the East Wing open only on occasional tour days, so don't plan around going inside.

**Our tip:** The park is free; the rink, rooftop Summit and Go Ape are ticketed, so book those ahead. Check the what's-on page, as the Great Hall hosts big events. From Wood Green it is a long uphill walk.
