It's on our list because it's the kind of small family place that survives on regulars rather than reputation. Mohsen has been on Warwick Road, across from the Olympia exhibition halls, since the mid-1990s. Reviews describe flatbread baked fresh, generous kebabs and the owner's family working the tables. The owners say it's a favorite with London's Iranian community, which we'd like to see for ourselves.

It doesn't sell alcohol, the prices are low for this part of west London, and it's handy if you're at an event at Olympia or staying in Earl's Court.

**Our tip:** Pace yourself on the fresh-baked bread; the kebabs are generous.
