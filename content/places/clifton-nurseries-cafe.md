This is one of our favorite places for a slow coffee and a piece of cake. Clifton Nurseries has been growing and selling plants in Little Venice since 1851 and calls itself London's oldest garden center. You reach it through gates squeezed between two houses, and the walk-in feels like finding a secret garden.

The café is a glasshouse with a terrace among the plants, and the nursery's Victorian-style glasshouse full of houseplants is next door. Expect brunch dishes, sandwiches, scones and cakes rather than a full meal. Service can be slow and it isn't cheap, but we keep going back for the setting. It's an easy add-on to a walk along the Regent's Canal from Little Venice.

**Our tip:** Combine it with the canal: walk from Little Venice toward Regent's Park, or have lunch with a view of the water at [Cafe Laville](place:cafe-laville).
