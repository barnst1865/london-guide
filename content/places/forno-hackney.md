A bakery, pasta shop and deli in a railway arch on Andrews Road, opened in 2023 by the team behind Ombra. It's known for maritozzi (cream-filled buns), Roman-style pizza and focaccia, and pastries. It now has other branches in Leytonstone and Mile End.

**Our tip:** It's a good stop before or after Columbia Road Flower Market on a Sunday. Weekends are busier than midweek.
