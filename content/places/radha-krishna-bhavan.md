It's on our list as the reason to ride the Northern line to Tooting, south London's long-established South Asian high street. Radha Krishna Bhavan has been on Tooting High Street for about 25 years, cooking Keralan food from the same family as Ragam in Fitzrovia. Reviews mention the cheerfully kitsch décor and regulars who have been coming for decades.

If you're coming this far, make an afternoon of it: Tooting Market and Broadway Market are a few minutes' walk away.

**Our tip:** The coconut seafood curries are what reviewers rave about; pair one with a dosa.
