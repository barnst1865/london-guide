A leisurely afternoon tea in the lounge of the Langham, which opened in 1865 as one of London's first grand hotels. The hotel says afternoon tea began here; the usual attribution is to the Duchess of Bedford in the 1830s or 1840s, though that is debated, so take the Langham's version as its own claim. It's a splurge and menus change with the seasons.

**Our tip:** Book ahead, and check the Langham's site for the current seasonal menu and any dress code.
