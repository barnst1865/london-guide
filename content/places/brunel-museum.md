The museum fills Marc Brunel's old engine house and looks down into the great shaft that started the Thames Tunnel. The Overground still runs through that tunnel, a few steps from the door. The tunnel itself can't be walked; the shaft and museum are the way in.

**Our tip:** Come on a weekend to catch the free guided tour, and check the site for opening days first, since they shrink in winter.
