The Tradescants were royal gardeners who brought new plants to England in the 1600s. The museum has a cafe and a courtyard garden.

**Our tip:** It's closed Mondays and on a few autumn dates, so check the hours page before going. Easy to pair with a walk along the river toward Westminster.
