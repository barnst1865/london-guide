Cross the footway on Barnes Bridge, turn upstream along The Terrace, and the White Hart is a couple of minutes on: a four-story Young's pub with balconies and riverside benches. The present building dates from 1899, but a pub has stood here much longer. Licensees are recorded from 1822, and local tradition, which can't be proven, goes back to 1662.

In the mid-19th century it was the headquarters of Barnes Football Club, one of the clubs that founded the Football Association in 1863. Today it's a comfortable, roomy riverside pub, with the last stretch of the Boat Race course right outside. This is where the Barnes version of our walk ends: Barnes Bridge station is around the corner.

**Our tip:** Check tide times before you set out. At the highest spring tides the river can flood the road and towpath along here for an hour or two.
