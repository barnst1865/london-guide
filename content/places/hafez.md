It's on our list because when Time Out asked Iranian Londoners for their favorite Persian restaurants, one replied, "you also have to mention Hafez in Bayswater, which has been around since the early '80s." It opened in 1983 and is still a family restaurant, with a bread oven turning out flatbread for the table and a menu of kebabs, saffron rice and slow-cooked stews.

It sits on a quiet street between Westbourne Grove and Notting Hill, so it's an easy dinner after Portobello Road. It's on the pricier side for this list.

**Our tip:** Order one of the stews (khoresh) alongside the kebabs; that's where Persian home cooking shows.
