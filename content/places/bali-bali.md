Indonesian restaurants are rare in London, and Bali Bali has been quietly cooking on Shaftesbury Avenue, right in the middle of theaterland, since 1986. The family behind it came to London from Padang in Sumatra, and two generations later it's still family-run: a small, friendly room of about ten tables, and a menu that's easy for first-timers. Families are made welcome.

The cooking leans toward Padang and Java: rich beef rendang, nasi goreng, satay with peanut sauce, gado gado and spicy sambal dishes. All the meat is halal. Prices are low for this part of town, which makes it a handy dinner before a show at the Palace or the Shaftesbury.

**Our tip:** Order the beef rendang and some satay to share, and book if you're going pre-theater, because the room is small.
