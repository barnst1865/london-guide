It's on our list because Wong Kei is less a restaurant than a piece of Chinatown history. It started as a cramped alleyway shop in the mid-1970s and has filled this five-storey building on Wardour Street since the 1980s. In a 2025 Time Out oral history, a regular of thirty years called it "one of the few deep rooted connections I have with my British-Chinese heritage," and others remember waiters shouting floor numbers in Cantonese.

The service is famously curt (that's part of the legend), the plates are cheap and the turnover is fast. Don't come for a long, romantic dinner.

**Our tip:** Go for roast meats over rice or a noodle soup, eat quickly and enjoy the theater.
