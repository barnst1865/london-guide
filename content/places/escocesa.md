Scottish seafood cooked and served the way a Spanish tapas bar would, with small plates at the counter and tables. It's from the same owner as Bar Esteban and has been on Church Street since about 2015.

**Our tip:** Weekday evenings only, with lunch at weekends. Check the booking page.
