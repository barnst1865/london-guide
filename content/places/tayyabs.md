Tayyabs is an East End institution and one of the best-value big nights out in London. Mohammed Tayyab arrived from Pakistan in 1964 and in the early 1970s opened a little café on Fieldgate Street serving tea and toast. His sons turned it into a Punjabi grill house that gradually swallowed the buildings next door, including an old pub and a 1907 building that once held a small synagogue. Today it seats hundreds, and it's noisy, crowded and great fun.

The lamb chops are the legend: marinated, charred and brought to the table still sizzling. Add a dry meat curry, a dal, the baby aubergines and a stack of naan. It's bring-your-own, and it's cheap enough that a big group can eat well for very little.

**Our tip:** Book, even midweek, and bring your own beer or wine. Order more lamb chops than you think you need.
