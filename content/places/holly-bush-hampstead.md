Historic England dates the stables to about 1797 and their conversion to about 1807, when the house became the Hampstead Assembly Rooms. We found no source for when it first sold beer, and CAMRA’s claim that the upstairs room was Romney’s studio isn’t confirmed elsewhere. The approach is uphill, with a step at the door.
