It's on our list because a Malaysian restaurant that has outlasted fifty years of London food fashions must be doing something right. Satay House opened in 1973, which makes it the longest-running Malaysian restaurant in the city, and the founding family's second generation now runs it. A 2009 review noted "plenty of Malaysian diners" in the room, which is the endorsement we trust most.

It's halal, inexpensive and tucked onto a side street a few minutes from Paddington, so it's an easy dinner if you're staying nearby or arriving off the Heathrow Express.

**Our tip:** Start with the satay (it's in the name for a reason), then the beef rendang.
