Cafe Laville has the best seat on the Regent's Canal. In the 1970s Westminster Council built a concrete raft over the western mouth of the Maida Hill Tunnel, which was dug in the 1810s, and put a café on top. Sit on the balcony and you're looking straight down the canal to Little Venice, with narrowboats sliding out of the tunnel beneath your feet.

We'll be honest: we come for the view more than the food, and it isn't cheap for what it is. But the pizza, fresh pasta and tiramisu are perfectly good, and it's a lovely, relaxed place for a long lunch or a drink with friends. It's a family favorite for exactly that reason. It also makes a natural start or finish for a walk along the towpath.

**Our tip:** Ask for a balcony table at the canal end, and come on a sunny afternoon.
