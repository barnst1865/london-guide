Grade II listed, with a frame of about 1688 or the early 1700s and a front rebuilt in 1958. The 1679 beating of the poet John Dryden in the alley nearby is documented; that Charles II ordered it is only alleged, and the prizefighting “Bucket of Blood” story is pub legend.
