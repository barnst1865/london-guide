According to the plaque, Arnold lived here from 1796 until his death in 1801. The plaque is not part of an official scheme; it was put up privately in 1987, and its 'American Patriot' wording is its own.

**Our tip:** Read the plaque from the pavement. It's a private building, so there's nothing to see inside.
