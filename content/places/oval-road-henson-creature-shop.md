The workshop where the Jim Henson Creature Shop built puppets and creatures until 2005 overlooked the Regent's Canal. It is now a private block of flats called The Henson.

**Our tip:** Pair it with a canal-side wander to Camden Lock, and keep to the public street and the towpath.
