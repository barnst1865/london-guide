A double-decker barge moored in Paddington Basin, run by the people behind The Cheese Bar. The menu is cheese-led small plates, cheese boards and toasties, with a wine and drinks bar. The terrace is a summer feature.

**Our tip:** The terrace is closed from October to March, so it's an indoor visit in winter. Check the website for booking terms.
