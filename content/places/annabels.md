Mark Birley opened Annabel's in 1963, and it moved to its Grade I listed Berkeley Square townhouse in 2018. The Queen dined there in 2003. It is members and guests only, so visitors can look from outside but not go in. The story goes the Beatles were once turned away for not wearing shoes; we can't source that one.

**Our tip:** Pair it with a stroll around Berkeley Square's plane trees.
