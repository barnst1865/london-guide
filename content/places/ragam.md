It's on our list because Fitzrovia is full of expensive restaurants and Ragam has quietly ignored all of them since 1985. It cooks the food of Kerala, India's southwestern coast: crisp dosas, coconut-rich curries and plenty for vegetarians. One reviewer from Kerala called it the best South Indian food they'd had in London, which is the kind of praise we look for.

It's small and unfussy, five minutes from Goodge Street, and an easy lunch between the British Museum and Regent's Park. Its sister restaurant, Radha Krishna Bhavan, is in Tooting.

**Our tip:** Order a masala dosa for the table while you decide on the curries.
