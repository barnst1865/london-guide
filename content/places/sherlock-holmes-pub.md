It has been the Sherlock Holmes since 1957, on the site of the old Northumberland Hotel that appears in the Holmes stories. The upstairs study is full of props from the stories.

**Our tip:** It sits a few minutes from Trafalgar Square, so it pairs easily with the National Gallery or a Thames walk.
