Gerrard Street is Chinatown's main drag, and it has plenty of tired tourist menus. Dumplings' Legend is the one we point people to. Brothers Geoff and Lucas Leong opened it in 2010, and the show is in the front window: a team of chefs in a glass-walled kitchen folding dumplings by hand all day long (thousands of them, by the restaurant's own count).

The thing to order is xiao long bao, the delicate soup dumplings from Shanghai, along with har gow and the rest of the dim sum list. There's a long menu of Sichuan and Cantonese dishes as well, so it works for a group with mixed tastes. It's busy and brisk rather than relaxing.

**Our tip:** Order the xiao long bao first. Lift each one gently onto a spoon, nibble a hole and sip the soup before eating it. Expect queues at weekends.
