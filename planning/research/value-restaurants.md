# Value restaurants: "The long-standing local" (research)

**Criteria:** Criteria set 1 in [criteria.md](criteria.md) · **Session:** 2026-10-03 · **Theme:** `eat` · **Status:** shortlist for triage, nothing drafted yet

**Thresholds used:** ~4.0+ with 200+ reviews (Google, or an equivalent count elsewhere) · £–££, with excellent ££+ places kept as near misses · broad cuisine spread, no cap · zones 1–4 · excluded anything already in `content/places/`, the Google Maps triage list or the office restaurant list (e.g. Tayyabs, Punjab, Veeraswamy, Bali Bali, Hijazi Corner, Samad Al Iraqi, Mon Plaisir, E Pellicci).

## How to read this

- **78 restaurants were checked.** Only 5 clear every criterion. The criterion that fails most often is #3, community patronage: plenty of places are old and well liked, but few sources say *who* eats there.
- **Google ratings:** Google Maps can't be fetched. Figures marked "G via Wanderlog" are the Google rating and count shown on Wanderlog's place page (mirrors Google). Otherwise the source is named: TA = Tripadvisor, OT = OpenTable. All seen 2026-10-03.
- **Same address:** "yes" = a source ties the opening date to this address; "likely" = the same address appears in sources across many years and nothing mentions a move, but no source from the opening year names it.
- **Price:** Harden's "£/head" figures include wine, service and coffee; food alone is roughly £20–25 less.
- **Research gap:** the session's web-search allowance ran out partway through, so several near misses are near misses for lack of a figure rather than a bad one. Many can be settled in seconds by looking the place up in Google Maps on a phone (rating, count, recent reviews, reviews in the community's language).
