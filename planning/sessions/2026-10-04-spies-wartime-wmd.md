## Corrections to the WMD draft (§8) and triage notes

- "Ryu Sushi" does not exist: the contaminated sushi bar was **Itsu, 167 Piccadilly** (visited 16 Oct with Lugovoi/Kovtun and 1 Nov with Scaramella). No source for polonium at the **British Library**: dropped. Litvinenko died at **UCH** (text only, not pinned); home not pinned.
- **PINDAR**: approved 1982–83, built from 1984, operational 7 Dec 1992, £126.3m (Hansard 29 Apr 1994). Not "1987".
- **Broad Street pump** reframed as epidemiology; replica 1992 → removed 2015 → reinstated on the original site 20 July 2018.
- **RAF Northolt** had no nuclear-bomber role: dropped; the **Polish Air Force Memorial** on the A40 is the place file instead.
- **BT Tower** was not "designed to withstand nuclear attack": a node in the Backbone network planned for nuclear resilience; cylinder for wind stability. OS-map myth noted. 1971 bomb claimed by the Angry Brigade, unsolved.
- **Markov** died at St James' Hospital, Balham (closed 1988, demolished); not St George's. Exact bus stop unknown: "south side". Umbrella labelled as unproven.
- **Skripal house was not demolished** (roof replaced 2019, bought by Wiltshire Council 2021, sold as shared ownership); Rowley's Amesbury flat was demolished from 2020. "Charity bin" not endorsed by the Dawn Sturgess Inquiry (reported 4 Dec 2025).
- **Scott's**: both 1975 attacks were at Mount Street (bomb 12 Nov; drive-by 6 Dec, not 12 Dec); moved 1967; Fleming stories belong to Coventry Street; martini claim apocryphal.
- "Blackfriars Bridge V-2 plaque": it is a 2007 railway-heritage plaque on Blackfriars Road (first station entrance) mentioning Blitz shrapnel and a Dec 1944 V-2; not a memorial. Not written up; mention only.
- Battle of Britain Monument unveiled 18 **September** 2005. Guards Chapel 121 killed/141 injured (civil/military split unverifiable, dropped). NAM gallery names updated (Formation, Soldier, Global Role, Conflict in Europe, Army at Home). IWM atrium has a V-2 but no V-1. Churchill's "so few" line first said leaving Uxbridge on 16 Aug 1940 (Ismay), Commons 20 Aug.
- Deferred spy rows: **GM104** Spy Lamp Post is in Audley Square, Mayfair (not Chelsea Embankment); whole story rests on Berkeley, *A Spy's London* (1994), written as "said to be". **GM107** "Blunt meets Guy Liddell" at Bentinck St unsupported: softened. **GM109** Brown's Hotel: no Blunt/MI5 link found → **not written**. **GM108** 30 Palace Court: no Blunt plaque exists (the only EH "Blunt" plaque is Wilfrid Scawen Blunt, Buckingham Gate) → **not written**. **GM112** Ivor Montagu / Ranelagh Gardens: no sourced address → **not written**. **GM113** Crabb birthplace verified (Streatham Society) → written at street precision. **GM114** 2 Carlton Gardens is the Institute for Government today (not government); No. 2 vs No. 3 for the SIS rooms left open in text. **GM111** Red Lion: no published Bossard source; written as "said to be" at the family's request; Bossard's arrest/sentence facts sourced.
