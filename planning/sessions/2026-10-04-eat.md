## Facts worth knowing before review

- **Violet's** (Soho) switched from a Mediterranean/Turkish café-bar to Georgian food around late 2025, and has had licensing disputes with Westminster Council (2021–25). The family visited after the change. The text doesn't mention the licensing history. Re-check its status at each sweep.
- **Regency Cafe** has new owners since mid-2025. Recent reviews mention higher prices and a ticket system instead of the famous shouted orders, so the text doesn't mention shouting.
- **Three Falcons** is in Lisson Grove (Church Street ward), not really St John's Wood; its area label says so.
- **Tamil Prince** is listed in the Michelin Guide but is not a Bib Gourmand (an earlier note was wrong).
- **Websites deliberately not linked:** E Pellicci (`epellicci.com` is now a spam site), Beigel Bake (no verified official site), and Sweetings, Regency Cafe, Cafe Laville and Dumplings' Legend (their sites wouldn't load when checked).
- **Chains** included with a stated §7 angle: Dishoom Battersea (the easiest Dishoom to get into) and Bread Street Kitchen Limehouse (a kid-friendly stop on a river walk).
