# 2026-10-04: Pin check

Checked map pins on public places marked `location_precision: exact`, in batches, and added `path_only` waypoints to the Thames Path trail. Directions now search Google Maps by name and address, so the pins matter mainly for the on-site maps and the trail lines.

## Method

- Coordinates came only from the OpenStreetMap object for the venue or its entrance (read through openstreetmap.org in the built-in browser), a Historic England listing, or the venue's own page. Nothing was guessed or nudged by eye.
- Confirmed: `coords` updated to 5 decimals, `exact` kept, the old centroid note replaced with `# Pin checked 2026-10-04: <source>`, address completed with a postcode.
- Not confirmable: set to `location_precision: street`, with the reason in a comment.
- Why `#` and not `<!-- -->`: the comment sits on the `coords:` line inside YAML front matter, where an HTML comment would break parsing.
- Google Maps and Google search were not usable from this session, so OSM was the main source.

## Scope covered

- Batches 1-3: the postcode-centroid group (60 places).
- Batches 4-7: public pubs, bars, restaurants, cafes and shops.
- Batches 8-9: large sites: museums (main visitor entrance), Hampstead Heath, Kensington Gardens (now Marlborough Gate), Kew Gardens, Richmond Park, Regent's Canal Walk (towpath access).
- Batch 10: 29 public historic sites, churches and memorials.

## Biggest corrections

- Churchill War Rooms (about 189 m), Crystal Palace Museum (196 m) and Young V&A (134 m) were well off.
- Billingsgate Market address is now Trafalgar Way, London E14 5ST.
- Most pubs and bars were already within about 60 m of the door.

## Trail: Thames Path, Hammersmith to Kew

13 `path_only` waypoints were added, taken from nodes of the OSM Thames Path relations: Dukes Meadows path (north, middle, south), Barnes Terrace towpath, Mortlake towpath (1)-(4), Kew towpath (1)-(3), Burlington Avenue (north, south). The Chiswick Bridge waypoint moved to the south landing. The other three trails were not changed (see requests).
